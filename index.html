How to read this page

The z-score compares each indicator's latest change with the spread of its own recent changes. Beyond roughly ±2, the move is unusual for that series - a prompt to look closer, not a buy or sell signal. Values are the latest observation each series has published, so dates differ (daily series are fresh; GDP is quarterly).
